# Glitch Pong — Short-Form Video Plan

Vertical videos for **TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts**. The 1:58 trailer
(`docs/trailer-plan.md`) is the full pitch. These are bite-sized hooks that each sell
**one** idea and send people to the game or the full trailer.

---

## Ground rules (apply to every short)

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps** | Native format on all three platforms. |
| **7–30 seconds** (most around 10–15s) | Short videos get rewatched, and rewatches push reach. All three platforms allow longer videos, but shorter performs better for hooks. |
| **Hook in the first second** | Start on motion plus a text hook. No logo intro, no fade-in. |
| **One idea per video** | "The ball gets tired" is one video. "The boss" is another. |
| **Burned-in captions** | Many people scroll with the sound off. Every beat must read without audio. |
| **Designed to work with sound too** | Use the game's own SFX and music, cut on the beat. |
| **Loop the ending** | The last frame should flow back into the first, so the video replays. |
| **Respect safe zones** | Keep text out of roughly the top 13%, the bottom 20% and the right-hand 10% of the frame, where platform buttons and captions sit. |
| **Logo last, for 1 second at most** | "GLITCH PONG — link in bio" on the final beat only. |

### Three vertical layouts

The game is a horizontal (landscape) court, so each short uses one of these:

- **A. Rotated court (full screen).** Rotate the court 90° so the paddles sit at the
  top and bottom. Blue at the bottom reads as "you". It fills the whole phone screen and
  feels like air hockey. Hide the sideways game HUD and add Heat/score back as upright
  overlays. *Best for: rallies, dashes, jukes, walls, power-ups.*
- **B. Stacked.** The 16:9 gameplay sits as a band across the middle (1080×608), with a
  big hook line above it and captions or a counter below. *Best for: menus, level-up,
  arena tours, side-by-side comparisons.*
- **C. Follow crop.** A 9:16 window that tracks the ball or the Eye. *Best for: the Eye
  close-up, the Drowsy Ball's Z's, last-pixel saves.*

## The formats

### 1. "Pong, but…" — the main series ⭐
**Length:** 10–15s · **Layout:** A · **Best on:** TikTok, Reels

The anchor series. Each episode takes one twist the game adds to Pong. The same title
template, posted again and again, builds recognition ("oh, it's the Pong-but guy again").

**Template**
| Time | Beat |
|------|------|
| 0:00–0:01 | Normal-looking rally. Hook text: **"Pong, but the ball gets tired"** |
| 0:01–0:08 | The twist builds (the rally gets longer, the ball wobbles, Z's appear) |
| 0:08–0:12 | Payoff moment (big reaction beat, often slow-mo) |
| 0:12–0:14 | Punchline caption, then a 1s end card. Cut back to a rally so it loops |

**Episodes** (each uses a real game feature)
1. **…the ball gets tired.** A 30-hit rally, then wobble, Z's float up and "BALL DROWSY" fires. Punchline: *"30 hits. It needed a nap."*
2. **…the ball holds a grudge.** A "LAST MISS" marker at the goal line, then the next serve goes exactly there. *"It remembers."*
3. **…the ball picks sides.** The score reads 5–1 and the ball curves toward the leader's weak spot. *"BALL SPITE ACTIVE."*
4. **…the ball can juke you.** The paddle commits, the ball flips direction at the last moment, and the freeze-frame lands on the miss. *"Ankles: broken."*
5. **…the walls glitch.** Corrupted walls flicker in, then the ball shatters one in slow-mo.
6. **…there's a black hole.** In Black-Hole Nexus, a micro black hole bends the ball into a score.
7. **…there's a boss.** The ball freezes, an angry bubble appears and it morphs into the Eye. Cut before the fight. *"Part 2?"* This sets up format 3.

### 2. "Which one is real?" — comment bait
**Length:** 10s, plus a reply video · **Layout:** A · **Best on:** TikTok, Shorts

| Time | Beat |
|------|------|
| 0:00–0:02 | Red grabs **Split**. Text: **"Only one of these is real."** |
| 0:02–0:03 | Three balls fan out |
| 0:03–0:07 | **Freeze**, with balls labelled **1 / 2 / 3** and the caption *"Comment 1, 2 or 3"* |
| 0:07–0:09 | Unfreeze. The paddle guesses wrong and the real ball scores (slow-mo) |
| 0:09–0:10 | *"Did you get it?"*, then loop |

**Variation:** post the freeze **without** the reveal, then reply to the top comment
with a reveal video (TikTok's "reply to comment" video). That's two posts from one clip.

### 3. "POV: you hit 100% Heat" — meme format
**Length:** 12–15s · **Layout:** C (Eye close-up), with A for the opening · **Best on:** TikTok, Reels

| Time | Beat |
|------|------|
| 0:00–0:02 | Heat meter in the 90s, frantic rally. Text: **"POV: you hit 100% Heat"** |
| 0:02–0:03 | **100%.** Hard audio cut to silence |
| 0:03–0:06 | Ball freezes, angry speech bubble |
| 0:06–0:09 | Glitches to center, morphs into the Eye. Follow crop pushes in |
| 0:09–0:11 | The Eye stares into the camera. *"It's been watching you."* |
| 0:11–0:14 | Red alarm drop and six orbital shots launch. Cut on the first shot |

### 4. "Rate this save 1–10" — highlight clips
**Length:** 7–10s · **Layout:** A or C · **Best on:** all three · **Cadence:** weekly

The cheapest format to keep making: one clutch moment per video.
- Last-pixel **Dash** save in slow-mo
- **Near miss** off the paddle tip
- **Shield** catching a shot that should have scored
- **Power Strike** return that rockets past the paddle

Text: **"Rate this save 1–10."** End on the hit, then loop to the start of the rally.
Asking for a number gets easy, high-volume comments.

### 5. "4 arenas, 1 game" — beat-synced tour
**Length:** 8–10s · **Layout:** B or A · **Best on:** TikTok, Reels (works with trending audio)

Two seconds per arena, hard cuts on the beat, arena name in big type:
**CLASSIC GRID → MATRIX MAINFRAME → THE CONSTRUCT → BLACK-HOLE NEXUS**, then back to
Classic so it loops. Use each arena's own music for the YouTube version. On TikTok and
Reels you can swap in a trending sound from the platform's own library.

### 6. "Normal Pong vs Glitch Pong" — comparison
**Length:** 10–12s · **Layout:** B (stacked twice) · **Best on:** Reels, Shorts

The top band shows a calm Classic Grid rally at 0% Heat, labelled **"Normal Pong"**.
The bottom band shows the same arena at 95% Heat with walls, decoys, teleports and a
red HUD, labelled **"Glitch Pong"**. At 0:08 the bottom ball becomes the Eye.
Caption: *"Same rules. Different game."* Use only your own Classic Grid footage for
the "normal" side.

### 7. Satisfying loops — no text needed
**Length:** 6–8s · **Layout:** C · **Best on:** Reels, Shorts

Perfect loops built for rewatches:
- A Glitch Wall shattering into pixel debris (slow-mo)
- The Drowsy Ball wobbling with Z's floating up
- The Black-Hole Nexus lensing with the ball spiralling past

Minimal or no text. Let the visuals and the SFX carry it.

### 8. "Survive the Eye" — challenge
**Length:** 20–30s · **Layout:** A, with C for the Eye · **Best on:** TikTok, Shorts

The full boss pattern with an on-screen counter: **"SHOTS SURVIVED 1/6 … 6/6"**.
Open with **"Can you survive all 6?"** and end on the resolve phase.
CTA: *"Stitch this with your run."* This invites player-made content.

### 9. Dev story — creator-led *(optional, needs you)*
**Length:** 20–40s · **Layout:** you in a corner over gameplay, or voiceover only · **Best on:** TikTok, Shorts

Indie devlogs perform well on short-form because people follow the creator, not only
the game. Ideas:
- *"I made Pong, but the arena fights back."* Show the reveal of each system.
- *"How I made the ball get tired."* 30-hit counter, wobble, Z's.
- *"My Pong game has a boss fight."* The Eye cinematic.

Speak to camera or record a voiceover. Keep it raw. Polish matters less here than personality.

### 10. Trailer cutdowns
**Length:** 15s and 30s · **Layout:** A + B mix · **Best on:** all three, and as ads

Vertical versions of the cutdowns already listed in `docs/trailer-plan.md`:
- **30s:** Glitch → countdown → DASH → SPLIT → Eye morph and stare → end card
- **15s:** calm rally → glitch → Eye morph and stare → GLITCH PONG → link

## Captions & hashtags

Keep captions to one line plus 3–5 hashtags. Examples:

| Format | Caption |
|--------|---------|
| Pong, but… | *pong, but the ball gets tired 💤* |
| Which one is real? | *only one of these is real. comment 1, 2 or 3* |
| POV 100% Heat | *never let the heat hit 100%* |
| Rate this save | *rate this save 1–10* |
| Survive the Eye | *6 shots. no mercy. can you survive?* |

**Hashtag pool** (mix 3–5 per post): `#GlitchPong` (always) `#IndieGame` `#IndieDev`
`#GameDev` `#Pong` `#RetroGaming` `#ArcadeGame` `#BrowserGame` `#Gaming`

### Platform notes
- **TikTok:** Use the "reply to comment" video for format 2. Trending sounds are fine
  at low volume under the game SFX, but only from TikTok's own library.
- **Instagram Reels:** Set a custom cover frame (the Eye close-up works well). Pin the
  best-performing Reel to the profile grid.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Keep the game's original audio, so copyrighted music doesn't
  trigger Content ID claims. Use the **related video** link on every Short to point at
  the full trailer.
- **Link in bio** on all three. Most clicks will come from phones, and the game is
  landscape-only, so add *"turn your phone sideways to play"* to the bio or a pinned
  comment, and check the mobile flow before posting.

## 4-week posting calendar

Post the same video to all three platforms, about 4 times a week.

| Week | Mon | Wed | Fri | Sun |
|------|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 1 | 30s trailer cutdown | Pong, but… *the ball gets tired* | Which one is real? | POV: you hit 100% Heat |
| 2 | Pong, but… *holds a grudge* | Rate this save (Dash) | 4 arenas, 1 game | Which one is real? (reply/reveal) |
| 3 | Pong, but… *can juke you* | Normal vs Glitch Pong | Satisfying loop (wall shatter) | Survive the Eye |
| 4 | Pong, but… *there's a boss* | Rate this save (Shield) | Dev story | 15s trailer cutdown |

After 2 weeks, check what's working and do more of it (see below).

## What to measure

| Metric | Good sign | If it's weak |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| **3-second hold** | Most viewers stay past 0:03 | Change the hook text and first frame |
| **Average % watched** | Above 70% for videos under 15s | Cut it shorter, remove slow setup |
| **Rewatches / loops** | Above 100% average view duration | Tighten the loop point |
| **Comments** | Format 2 and 4 get the most | Ask a clearer question on screen |
| **Profile visits / link clicks** | Rising each week | Make the end card and CTA clearer |

## Production notes

Most shots can come from the existing renderer in `trailer/` (staged shots already
exist for Dash, Juke, Near Miss, Shield, Power Strike, Split, Drowsy Ball, Last Miss,
the arenas and the Eye). Adding short-form needs:

1. **A 9:16 output mode** in the renderer: rotate (layout A), stack (B) and follow-crop (C).
2. **Overlays drawn after rotation**, so text stays upright in layout A.
3. **Safe-zone guides** in the contact sheets, to check text placement.
4. **A few new captures:** the "Survive the Eye" counter run, the Normal-vs-Glitch pair,
   slow-mo wall shatters for loops, and the 1/2/3 labels for Split.
5. **One script per short**, rendering `out/shorts/<name>.mp4` at 1080×1920.
