HOW TO PLAY

SPACE / CLICKHold to climb. Let go to fall. That is the only control.
P / ESCPause.

There is no paddle. You are the ball, in orbit round a hole in the machine's memory. Collect the white bits to salvage the sector. Lower orbits are faster and worth more: x2 in the middle band, x3 next to the hole.

Everything else in orbit is what the other two cabinets threw away. Touch it and the ball is gone. Fall past the horizon and the ball is gone. Passing close scores, and raises Heat.

Heat multiplies your score up to x3. It also widens the hole, brings more junk, and gets you noticed.

Each sector has one rule. Every fifth sector, THE EYE looks through the hole: thread the gap in its gaze, and take the pink charges while it is open.

A sector pays 2 coins at the prize counter. THE EYE pays 10.
